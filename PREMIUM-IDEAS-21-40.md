# 🥇 TOP 5 — INSE SABSE ZYADA PAISA AAYEGA

## 21. 💰 UDHAAR KHATA (Dukaan ka udhaar + WhatsApp reminder)

**Kya karega:** Dukaan wala grahak ka naam + udhaar likhega.
Bot **poori list rakhega**, aur jab mahina pura ho — bot **khud WhatsApp message
taiyaar kar dega**: *"Namaste Ramesh ji, aapka ₹1,250 udhaar baaki hai…"*
+ mahine ka hisaab **PDF me print** ke liye.
**Kaun kharidega:** har kirana, medical, kapde ki dukaan, sabzi wala, bakery
**Kyun paise dega:** dukaan wale ka **sabse bada dard** yahi hai — udhaar bhool jata hai,
paisa doob jata hai. Ye tool **seedha-cash** bachata hai.
**Paisa:** ₹499/saal (per dukaan). Ek dukaan se 10 dukaan tak aasan bik jayega.
**Kaam:** 🟢 aasan — database + PDF + WhatsApp text. Aapke bot me list/PDF system pehle se hai.
**Sifarish:** **SABSE PEHLE YEHI BANAO** — sabse zyada bikne wala.

## 22. 📑 LEGAL DRAFT PACK (Shapath Patra / Affidavit + Legal Notice)

**Kya karega:** naam, pita ka naam, address, jila daalo → bot **tayyar kagaz** de de:
- Shapath Patra (affidavit) — naam badalna, jaati, aay, gap year, address proof
- Legal Notice (paisa wasooli / cheque bounce / dukaan ka jhagda)
- NOC / Consent letter / Authority letter
**Kaun kharidega:** aam aadmi, dukaan wale, students (gap certificate), property wale
**Kyun paise dega:** vakil/notary **₹200-500 leta hai ek kagaz ka**. Bot **₹0 me 1 minute** me.
**Paisa:** ₹99 per kagaz · ₹499 = 10 kagaz pack
**Kaam:** 🟢 aasan — bas templates (jo main likh dunga) + user ka data bhar dena
**Zaroori:** neeche chhota disclaimer lagega — *"ye draft hai, final vakil/notary se jaanch karae"*

## 23. 🌾 KISAN PACK (Kheti ka poora hisaab)

**Kya karega:**
- **Fasal ka munafa:** beej + khaad + mazdoori kharcha daalo → ek bigha se kitna munafa, PDF
- **Mandi bhav** ka record (aap khud roz daal denge ya user apne mandi ka bhav likhega)
- **Kheti ka khata:** kharid/becha → saal ka hisaab
- **Fasal beema (PMFBY) ke kagaz** — form + patta
**Kaun kharidega:** kisan, kheti ka thekedaar, tractor wale, beej-khaad dukaan
**Kyun paise dega:** kisan ko **pata hi nahi hota** ki uski fasal me munafa hai ya nahi.
Ye tool saal bhar kaam aata hai.
**Paisa:** ₹199 per fasal (saal me 2-3 baar = **repeat paisa**) · ₹499/saal unlimited
**Kaam:** 🟡 thoda kaam — calculator + PDF, par simple hai
**Bihar/UP me market:** bahut bada 🚜

## 24. 🎬 PHOTO SE VIDEO (Shaadi / Birthday / Status video)

**Kya karega:** 10-30 photo + ek Hindi gaana bhejo → bot **video bana de**
(photo ek-ek karke aayengi, gaane ke saath, naam/title ke saath) — Instagram/WhatsApp status ready
**Kaun kharidega:** shaadi-tilak me har ghar, photo studio wale, birthday, school annual function
**Kyun paise dega:** video editor **₹500-2000 leta hai**. Aapka bot **1 minute** me.
+ **Status video** har hafte log banate hain
**Paisa:** ₹99 per video · ₹499 = 8 video · studio wale ko ₹1,499/saal unlimited
**Kaam:** 🟡 thoda kaam — `ffmpeg` aapke bot me **pehle se hai** (video trim/compress/ringtone chal rahe hain)

## 25. 🛒 SELLER KIT (Amazon / Flipkart / Meesho par bechne walon ke liye)

**Kya karega:** order ki list Excel me daalo → bot **sab taiyaar kar de**:
- **Shipping label** (naam, address, pin, phone — sahi size me)
- **Packing slip** + **Invoice (GST)** + **Return slip**
- Ek order ke 4 kagaz, ek hi PDF me — **print karke chipka do, kaam khatam**
**Kaun kharidega:** Meesho/Amazon reseller — **har mohalle me 5-10 log** ye kaam karte hain
**Kyun paise dega:** wo rozana 20-50 parcel bhejte hain. Market me ye plugins **₹500-1000/mahina**.
**Paisa:** ₹999/saal (Seller Pack) · ya ₹99/month
**Kaam:** 🟡 thoda kaam — PDF/label layout + Excel import (openpyxl pehle se hai)
