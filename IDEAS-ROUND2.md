  ```
  BANKIPUR (182) Bihar — WON: PRASHANT KISHOR (Jan Suraaj Party)

  ├ 3rd: REKHA KUMARI (RJD) — 14273
  └ poori candidate-wise list + EVM round count
  ```
- **Key:** ❌ **koi nahi** · **Legal:** ECI ka khud ka public result portal
- **Bot me kya banega:** `🗳️ CHUNAV X-RAY` → `Bankipur` bhejo → winner + top 5 candidates +
  margin. Election ke din **"Kitna count hua"** refresh karta rahega.

  users roz check karenge — bot viral hone ka mauka 🚀
