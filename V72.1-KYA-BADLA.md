## 1. 🎯 GK Quiz Challenge — roz 5 sawal

- **Roz ka quiz:** har din **5 naye sawal** — sawal **aapke hisaab se** tay hote hain
  (aapka ID + aaj ki tareekh se) → purane user ko bhi roz naya quiz.
- **Sawal ka pattern:** Bihar GK + India GK + Science + Sports mix — 91 sawalon ka bank.
- **Har jawab ke baad:** sahi/galat + **chhota sa fact** (jo yaad rakhne layak ho) —
  gyaan bhi milta hai, sirf score nahi.
- **Aakhir me:** score (jaise 4/5), % , aur tagline (Topper / Bahut achha / Aur mehnat).
- **🔥 Streak:** roz khelne par streak badhta hai (2 din, 3 din...) + **🏆 Best score**
  yaad rehta hai. Beech ka din chhoota to streak phir 1 se shuru — **roz khelne ka maza**.
- **📚 "Aur 5 practice sawal":** roz ka quiz ho gaya? Ek tap me 5 random practice sawal —
  practice wale streak me nahi ginte (ye jaan-boojh ke — warna streak ka matlab nahi rahta).

### Kaise dikhta hai (sample)
```
┏────────────────────────────┓
┃ 🎯 GK QUIZ CHALLENGE
┗────────────────────────────┛

❓ Sawal 1 · Score: 0

🧠 Bihar ki rajdhani kya hai?

A) Gaya
B) Patna
C) Muzaffarpur
D) Bhagalpur
```
Buttons: **A) Gaya | B) Patna** (2×2 grid) + ⏹️ Chhodo (beech me chhodna bhi safe).

## 3. 📊 Marks Calculator — % + grade + division

Number bhejo, seedha jawab. **Teen tareeke chalte hain:**

| Aap likhein | Matlab | Kya milega |
|---|---|---|
| `350/500` | obtained/total | 70.00% • Grade B2 • First Division 🥇 |
| `80 75 90 66` | subject-wise marks | total 400 • 77.75% • Grade B1 • First |
| `chahiye 60 500 200` | 60% chahiye, 500 me se 200 aaye hain | kitne aur chahiye, ya "target pehle hi pura!" |

- **Grade:** A1 se E tak (CBSE pattern), saath me chhota matlab (Achha / Bahut achha...).
- **Division:** First 🥇 / Second 🥈 / Third 🥉 / Fail.
- **"chahiye" mode:** exam se pehle kaam aata hai — *"pass ke liye kaafi 300 marks, aur 100 chahiye."*
- Galat/adhoora input par **saaf message + 1 example** — crash ya khaali card kabhi nahi.
