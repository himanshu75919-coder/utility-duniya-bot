# 1️⃣ TEMP NUMBER — "app/website login ho jaye" (POORA SACH)

## Aapke bot me abhi kya hai
`🌐 VIRTUAL NUMBERS (OTP)` — service + desh chuno → **admin (aap) manually number dete ho**.
Ye "service" model hai. Ab aage kya ho sakta hai, teen raste:

| Rasta | Login me chalta hai? | Kharcha | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. FREE public numbers** (receive-smss jaisi sites) | ⚠️ **Chhote sites par 40-50%** · WhatsApp/Instagram/Gmail **~10%** (block kar dete hain) | ₹0 | ⚠️ **Inbox PUBLIC hai** — koi bhi OTP padh sakta hai → account hijack! |
| **B. PAID private numbers** (sms-activate / 5sim — real SIM pool) | ✅ **Zyadatar apps par chalta hai** (WhatsApp/Telegram/Insta bhi) | ₹5–30 per OTP | Aapke bot me **auto** ho sakta hai (API hai) par: platform ToS + account ban risk |
| **C. Apna dusra SIM / eSIM** | ✅ **100% chalta hai, hamesha** | ₹150–200/mahina | Sabse safe — **aur yahi main recommend karta hoon** |

## 🚫 RED LINES (inme temp number = CRIME, aur main nahi banaunga)
- ❌ Bank / UPI / Paytm / PhonePe / KYC / Aadhaar-linked apps — **fraud** hai
- ❌ Kisi aur ka account banana / OTP intercept / "kisi ka number pakadna"
- ❌ Kisi ko bech ke "ye permanent number hai" bolna (warna aapke bot ki badnami)

## ✅ Jo main BANA SAKTA hoon (aap pick karo)
1. **🔓 TEMP NUMBER (AUTO)** — paid API (sms-activate) se: user credits se number khareede →
   OTP bot me hi aaye → **VIP earning feature** (aapke competitor ye ₹30-50 me bechte hain 😏)
   → VIP only + **saaf likha ho: "sirf testing/scratch account ke liye — bank/UPI/KYC par KABHI nahi"**
2. **⚠️ FREE pool wala** — public sites se free numbers + unka public inbox dikhao, **badi warning ke saath**
   ("koi bhi OTP padh sakta hai — important account me mat use karo")
3. **📘 GUIDE upgrade** — existing vnum tool me: "kaunsa app temp number leta hai" ka honest table
   + "asli kaam ke liye dusra SIM lo" advice

# 2️⃣ "HACKING" TOOLS — jo main **NAHI** banaunga vs jo **LEGAL** hai

## ❌ NAHI (ye crime hai — bot ban + jail ka khatra)
WhatsApp/Facebook **hack karna**, kisi ka **CCTV** dekhna, **OTP intercept**, **phone camera access**,
"ghar ka wifi password todna", kisi ke account ka **access lena** — in sab ka koi "tool" nahi banaunga.
Jo bhi "hacking tool" free me mil raha hai na — **99% malware/scam** hota hai (aapka hi data chura leta hai).
