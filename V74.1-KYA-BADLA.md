## 1) Render ka "No open ports detected" warning — FIX ✅

**Problem:** Render log me warning aa rahi thi — "no open ports detected".
Agar Render ko port nahi milta, to free service **so jaati hai** (bot band ho jaata hai).

**Ab kya hota hai:**
- Bot apne aap port dhoondta hai: `PORT` ➜ `RENDER_PORT` ➜ `10000` (jo mile).
- Port bind fail ho jaye to **3 baar koshish** karta hai (3-3 second ke gap se) — kachra purana port band karke naya leta hai.
- Server aise banaya hai ki address turant reuse ho sake (Render ka scan turant pass ho jaye).
- Log me saaf likha aata hai: `Keepalive server listening on port XXXX (bind try N) — Render scan OK`.

**Matlab:** Render ki scan hamesha pass hogi, bot 24 ghante jagta rahega. Neend ka tension khatam. 😴➜☀️
