## 🎯 Aapki har shart — ab kya hota hai

| Aapne kaha | Ab kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| "Pichla message sab delete karo" | **Ho gaya.** Jis second aapko number milta hai, bot us waqt ke saare SMS ko "purana" mark kar deta hai. Purane SMS **kabhi show nahi honge**. |
| "Fresh number laao" | **Ho gaya.** Naya system (v2) me sabko naye numbers mil gaye — aap bhi button dabate hi **fresh number** paoge. |
| "Sirf mera OTP rahe, dusre ka nahi" | **Ho gaya.** Sirf wahi SMS dikhte hain jo **aapka number milne ke BAAD** aaye. |
| "Other websites ka nahi chahiye" | **Ho gaya.** Aapne jo app chuna (jaise WhatsApp), uske naam wale SMS hi dikhte hain. Dusri site wale chhup jaate hain (card me sirf ginti dikhti hai: "🔇 3 naya SMS chhupa"). |
| "6 digits ka OTP aaye, 4 digits nahi" | **Ho gaya.** Har app ka apna rule: WhatsApp = **6 digit**, Telegram = **5-6**, Google = **6 (G-xxxxxx)**, Netflix = **4-6**. Galti se 4-digit mila to bot saaf likhta hai: "is SMS ka code 4 digit ka hai — WhatsApp ka OTP nahi lagta". |
| "OTP system upgrade karo — best service ke accordingly" | **Ho gaya.** Letter-mixed code (Google ka `G-F9u0R6`) bhi pakadta hai, phone number/URL ke digits ko OTP nahi samajhta. |
| "Best service ke accordingly number" | **Ho gaya.** Ab bot pehle 3 numbers ka inbox khud check karta hai aur wahi number deta hai jis par **abhi-abhi usi app ke SMS** aa rahe hon (purani soch: koi bhi number). |

## 🧊 Number kaise use karo (naya flow)

1. **📞 TEMP MAIL (NUMBER)** → app chuno (WhatsApp) → desh chuno
2. Bot number dega + likhega: *"Abhi koi naya OTP nahi aaya"*
3. Number app me daalo (OTP maangte waqt)
4. Jo SMS **aapke liye** aaya, usko dekhne ke liye **🔁 Naya OTP check karo** dabao
5. Card me sabse upar bada OTP dikhega: `🔑 OTP: 556677`

> Purane SMS / dusri site wale SMS **kabhi nahi dikhenge** — bas ginti dikhegi.

## ⚠️ Jo nahi badla (safety waisi hi)

- 🚫 **Bank / UPI / KYC / paisa** wale OTP ke liye ye number **bilkul mat do** — intro + har card me warning.
- Inbox me kahin bank/UPI ka SMS dikha to bot ⛔ **RUKO** warning deta hai (chhupa hua SMS ho to bhi).
- Ye **public** number hai — inbox duniya me koi bhi dekh sakta hai.
- Tool **100% FREE** hai — koi credit/paisa nahi (premium tools 37 hi hain).
- Purana **📧 TEMP MAIL** (email wala) aur **🌐 VIRTUAL NUMBERS** jaisa tha waisa hi hai — kuch delete nahi hua.
