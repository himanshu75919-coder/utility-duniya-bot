### Numbers kahan se aate hain (100% free, koi login nahi)

3 public "receive SMS" sites se: **receivesms.co** (primary), **receive-sms-free.cc**, **temp-sms.org**.
Wahi kaam jo browser me wahi page kholne par hota hai — koi key/OTP chori nahi.
Sites par spam na ho, isliye: cache (12s) + rate-limit (25/5min) + dheeme requests.

### ⚠️ Saaf-saaf warning (jaisa aapne kaha)

- Ye **public** number hai — iska inbox duniya me koi bhi dekh sakta hai.
- Sirf **ek-baar ke OTP** (signup/verification) ke liye.
- **Bank / UPI / KYC / paise** wale OTP ke liye ye number **bilkul mat** dena.
- Agar kisi SMS me bank/UPI jaisa word dikha, bot turant **⛔ RUKO** warning deta hai.
