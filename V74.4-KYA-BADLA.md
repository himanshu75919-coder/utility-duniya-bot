## 2) 🔧 Webhook "error" — permanent ilaaj

Aapke Render logs me jo **WARNING** line thi (`WEBHOOK MODE | polling OFF`) — wo error nahi thi, par dikhti darawani thi. Ab:
- **WARNING → INFO**, saaf text: `WEBHOOK MODE ON — polling OFF (Conflict ka koi chance nahi)`.
- **NAYA: Webhook watchdog** — bot har **15 minute** khud check karta hai ki Telegram me webhook theek hai kya. Agar URL badal gaya / koi error aaya / messages atak gaye → **khud dobara set kar deta hai (3 retry ke saath)**. User ko pata bhi nahi chalta. **Ye permanent ilaaj hai.**

## 3) 📡 Boards ke liye — auto-watch (aapke "saare boards live" ke liye)

Aaj bhi saare reachable boards dobara probe kiye (UP, JAC, BSEH, PSEB, GSEB, MBOSE, MBSE, BBOSE, Odisha, HPBOSE, CISCE, NIOS):

- **IndiaResults wale (Mizoram/Nagaland/Tripura/Meghalaya):** page par roll form hi nahi hai

**Par ab system khud dekhta rahega:** naya **BOARD WATCH** — bot har **6 ghante** khud in boards ke result page check karta hai. Jis din koi board **khul jaye** (roll form mile + captcha na ho) → aapko (owner ko) Telegram par message aa jayega: *"📡 ye board khula mila — live add kar dun?"* — usi din LIVE ho jayega. **Jhootha live hum kabhi nahi dikhayenge** (aapka hi rule: "no any wrong").
